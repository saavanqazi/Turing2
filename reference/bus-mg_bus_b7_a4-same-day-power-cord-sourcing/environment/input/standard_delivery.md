# Kestrel — standard delivery options (not same-day)

| option | arrives | fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard ground | 4-6 business days | free over USD 25.00, otherwise USD 4.99 |
| Two-day | 2 business days | USD 5.99 |
| Ship to store | 3-5 business days | free |
| Store transfer | next business day | USD 2.99 |

Standard options are cheaper than same-day fulfilment and are quoted at the online price
with tax charged at the delivery address. They are shown here for comparison only; terms
S1 to S7 say which offers a same-day order may use.
